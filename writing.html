⏱ 1. Eksamensramme, Tidsstyring & Vurderingskriterier
Exam Framework, 4-Phase Time Allocation & Official SIRI Assessment Criteria
Total Duration: 4 Hours (240 min)

The written exam (Skriftlig fremstilling) lasts a total of 4 hours (240 minutes) and consists of two mandatory parts: Delprøve 1 (the informal personal email) and Delprøve 2 (either 2A statistics/analysis or 2B debate/opinion essay).

Anbefalet Tidsallokering (Recommended 4-Phase Time Budget)

Phase 1 · 15 Minutes
Forberedelse & Disposition
Carefully read both prompts, choose definitively between 2A and 2B, identify every single sub-question, and write a bulleted disposition/outline before writing full sentences.
Phase 2 · 45–50 Minutes
Delprøve 1 – E-mail
Draft the informal email using the 4-step modular blueprint. Directly and empathetically address every question from the prompt in its own distinct paragraph.
Phase 3 · 80–90 Minutes
Delprøve 2 – 2A eller 2B
Write either 2A (graph analysis) or 2B (debate essay). Strictly enforce the 4-paragraph model, ensuring Point 3 receives ~50% of the essay length for maximum score.
Phase 4 · 20–25 Minutes
Korrektur & Finpudsning
Systematic proofreading line by line. Never leave early! Run the 5-point grammatical checklist: V2 inversion, S-C-V-O in ledsætninger, nutids-r, adjective endings, and compound nouns.

De 4 Officielle Vurderingskriterier (The 4 Official SIRI Evaluation Rubrics)

1. Opgaveløsning & Kommunikation
Are all bullet points in the prompt exhaustively answered? Is the register hit accurately (warm and personal for Delprøve 1 vs objective, factual, and academic for Delprøve 2)?
2. Struktur & Tekstsammenhæng (Kohæsion)
Is the text logically structured into clear paragraphs? Are discourse connectors used effectively to create an unbroken red thread (rød tråd) rather than isolated blocks?
3. Ordforråd & Idiomatik
Does the candidate demonstrate a varied, nuanced B2 vocabulary with accurate collocations and fixed Danish idioms, avoiding repetitive basic verbs and unidiomatic calques?
4. Grammatisk Korrekthed
Consistent command of core Danish syntax: V2 rule (inversion in main clauses), subordinate clause order (S-C-V-O), correct adjective inflection (-t / -e), and present tense nutids-r.

✉️ 3. Delprøve 1: Den Uformelle E-mail (Råd, Svar & Relation)
Modular 4-Step Blueprint, Plug-and-Play Scaffold, Authentic Model & Inversion Anchor

In Delprøve 1, you write a personal email to a friend, colleague, or study partner who faces a specific life situation and asks for your advice, experience, or help. The goal is to demonstrate natural, empathetic communication while exhaustively addressing all prompt questions.

Den Modulære 4-Trins Struktur (The 4-Step Architecture)

  • Trin 1 (Indledning & Kontakt): Friendly opening ('Hej [Navn]') followed by an appreciative acknowledgement ('Tak for din mail! Det var godt at høre fra dig.').
  • Trin 2 (Personlig Status): Short, natural update on your everyday life, study, or job to establish authentic context.
  • Trin 3 (Systematisk Besvarelse): One separate paragraph per question in the prompt. Introduce each paragraph with an explicit topic marker ('Mht. X...', 'Når det kommer til Y...', 'Du spørger også om...').
  • Trin 4 (Afslutning & Fremadrettet CTA): Concrete proposal for meeting or next contact ('Hvad siger du til, at vi mødes...?') followed by standard informal sign-off.
⚓ Grammatisk Anker 1: Inversion & Forfelt i Delprøve 1 (V2 Rule)

The Golden Danish Syntax Rule: In Danish main clauses, the finite verb MUST strictly occupy Position 2. When starting a sentence with a transition phrase like 'Mht. X' or a conditional/time clause like 'Når det kommer til Y', that entire phrase occupies the FORFELT (Position 1).

KORREKT Mht. dit studiejob (1) tror (2) jeg (S) ikke, det vil skade dine karrieremuligheder.
FORKERT Mht. dit studiejob jeg tror ikke... (English word order costs massive points!)
Talesprogsvarianten 'så': If you insert 'så' after the forfelt, 'så' acts as a connector marker, and the finite verb must follow immediately after: 'Mht. studiejob så har (V) jeg (S) lige fået...'

📊 4. Delprøve 2A: Statistik- og Analyse-essayet
Modular 4-Pillar Model, Authentic Model & Causality Anchor

In Delprøve 2A, you analyze a statistical chart or bar graph. The text must be objective, neutral, and well-structured. You must NEVER use personal anecdotes or write 'jeg synes'. The essay is built on 4 mandatory pillars: 1) Data description, 2) Causal explanations, 3) Evaluation of advantages, and 4) Evaluation of disadvantages & societal consequences.

⚓ Sprogligt Anker 2: Kausalitet, Passiv & Data i Delprøve 2A

Delprøve 2A demands an academic register characterized by precise causal conjunctions, statistical precision, and passive constructions:

  • Årsag & Virkning (Cause & Effect): 'på grund af' (+ substantivisk led / noun phrase), 'fordi / da / eftersom' (+ ledsætning), 'hvilket medfører / resulterer i...'
  • Forklarende præcisering (Clarification): 'nemlig' (explains what immediately preceded), 'med andre ord' (reformulates into core essence).
  • Passiv for objektiv tone (Passive Voice): Employ the Danish s-passiv instead of personal pronouns: 'holdes der liv i...' (instead of 'så holder de liv i'), 'skoler nedlægges...'
  • Talbeskrivelse (Data Precision): 'steg fra X til Y', 'udgør langt størstedelen', 'en markant stigning på Z procentpoint'.

Modeltekst: Delprøve 2A (Folkeskoler vs Friskoler/Privatskoler)

Opgavestruktur & Vejledning Modeltekst & English Translation
Punkt 1: Beskrivelse af hovedtræk
(Data description: population, change, baseline majority) 		Diagrammet viser andelen af elever, der gik i henholdsvis folkeskoler og privat- eller friskoler i skoleårene 2007/2008 og 2022/2023. Ifølge diagrammet steg andelen af elever, der gik i privat- eller friskoler, fra cirka 10 % til cirka 20 %. Dog gik langt størstedelen af de danske børn stadig i folkeskoler i 2023, nemlig omkring 80 %. The diagram shows the proportion of pupils who attended public schools (folkeskoler) and private or independent schools (privat- eller friskoler), respectively, in the school years 2007/2008 and 2022/2023. According to the diagram, the proportion of pupils attending private or independent schools increased from approximately 10% to approximately 20%. Nevertheless, the vast majority of Danish children still attended public schools in 2023, namely around 80%.
Punkt 2: Årsagsforklaring
(At least 2 plausible causes for the development)

Der kan være flere årsager til, at flere forældre i de seneste år har sendt deres børn i en fri grundskole. En årsag er, at der er lukket mange kommunale folkeskoler på grund af faldende elevtal, presset økonomi eller effektiviseringer. Når lokale skoler nedlægges, går forældregrupper nogle gange sammen og åbner i stedet en friskole. På den måde holdes der liv i lokalsamfundene, og børnene undgår lang transport til en anden skole.

There can be several reasons why more parents in recent years have sent their children to an independent primary school. One reason is that many municipal public schools have closed due to falling student numbers, financial pressures, or efficiency measures. When local schools are closed down, parent groups sometimes join forces and open an independent school instead. In this way, life is sustained in local communities, and the children avoid long commutes to another school.

En anden årsag kan være, at forældrene er utilfredse med forholdene i folkeskolen. Det kan eksempelvis være mange elever i klasserne og lange skoledage. Det er faktorer, som kan medføre mere uro, dårligere trivsel og mindre læring.

Another reason may be that parents are dissatisfied with conditions in the public school. For example, there may be crowded classes and long school days. These are factors that can lead to more disruption, poorer well-being, and less learning.
Punkt 3: Vurdering af fordele
(50% Rule Pt. 1: Evaluation of advantages) 		Der kan være både fordele og ulemper ved at vælge en fri- eller privatskole frem for en folkeskole. En vigtig fordel er, at forældrene har større indflydelse på, hvilken slags undervisning deres børn modtager i skolen. Mange frie grundskoler bygger nemlig på nogle bestemte værdier eller en bestemt pædagogisk tænkning. Ved at sende deres børn i en fri- eller privatskole kan forældrene således vælge en skole, der passer til deres egne værdier og ønsker. Mange af skolerne har eksempelvis undervisningstilbud, der fokuserer på musik, leg og fællesskab. There can be both advantages and disadvantages in choosing an independent or private school over a public school. An important advantage is that parents have greater influence over the type of education their children receive in school. Many independent schools are based on specific values or distinct pedagogical philosophies. By sending their children to an independent or private school, parents can thus choose a school that aligns with their own values and wishes. Many of the schools, for instance, offer teaching programs focusing on music, play, and community.
Punkt 3: Vurdering af ulemper
(50% Rule Pt. 2: Evaluation of drawbacks & societal consequences)

På den anden side kan der også være nogle ulemper. En ulempe er, at forældrene skal betale for deres børns skolegang. Det kan være en stor omkostning for forældrene, særligt hvis de har flere børn. Derudover kan det udelukke børn af forældre, der ikke har råd til selv at betale. Dermed risikerer man, at der kommer a- og b-skoler i samfundet, fordi mange af eleverne med ressourcestærke forældre går på privatskoler, mens de mere ressourcesvage går i folkeskolen.

On the other hand, there can also be some disadvantages. One drawback is that parents must pay tuition for their children's education. This can be a heavy financial burden for parents, especially if they have multiple children. Furthermore, it can exclude children of parents who cannot afford to pay. Thereby, one risks the emergence of "tier-A" and "tier-B" schools in society, because many students with privileged parents attend private schools, while the less privileged attend public schools.

En anden ulempe er, at eleverne i de frie grundskoler i høj grad kun møder børn, der er som dem selv. I folkeskolen er der derimod stor mangfoldighed med børn fra forskellige kulturelle baggrunde og samfundslag. Med andre ord er hele samfundet repræsenteret, og det er vigtigt for børnene, for de skal lære at arbejde sammen med forskellige typer af mennesker.

Another disadvantage is that pupils in independent schools largely encounter only peers similar to themselves. In the public school, by contrast, there is wide diversity with children from varied cultural backgrounds and social strata. In other words, society as a whole is represented, and this is crucial for children, as they must learn to collaborate with diverse kinds of people.
💬 5. Delprøve 2B: Det Samfundsdebatterende Holdningsessay
Modular 4-Paragraph Blueprint, Authentic Model & Nuance Anchor

In Delprøve 2B, you discuss a current societal topic based on a set of statements provided in the exam prompt. The exam requires balanced argumentation connecting the broad topic to your own observations, taking an explicit stance on a quote, and evaluating pros and cons of a proposed measure.

⚓ Sprogligt Anker 3: Argumentation & Nuancering i Delprøve 2B

Sophisticated B2 argumentation deliberately avoids flat spoken formulas like 'Jeg synes det er dårligt'. Instead, employ high-scoring parenthetical constructions, balanced connectors, and causal chains:

  • Indskudt vurdering (Parenthetical stance): 'Det, mener jeg, er rigtigt.' (Strict commas and V2 word order!).
  • Balanceret modstilling (Balanced opposition): 'På den ene side... På den anden side...'
  • Konsekvenskæder (Chains of causation): '...for den gør det svært samtidig at leve op til...' / 'Dette kan medføre, at...'
  • Præcisering (Analytical precision): 'Man prøver med andre ord at reducere X ved at gøre Y, hvilket strider imod...'

Modeltekst: Delprøve 2B (Kønskvoter og Kvindelige Ledere)

Opgavestruktur & Vejledning Modeltekst & English Translation
Punkt 1: Fortællende afsnit
(Narrative opening: societal premise + workplace example) 		Der er en høj grad af ligestilling i Danmark, men det halter på nogle områder. Eksempelvis er der langt flere mandlige end kvindelige topledere. Det er også tilfældet i det firma, hvor jeg arbejder. Det er et privat firma, der sælger IT-udstyr. Firmaet har afdelinger i både Danmark og andre lande, og der er omkring 200 medarbejdere. Selvom cirka 20 % af alle de ansatte er kvinder, er der ingen kvindelige ledere eller bestyrelsesmedlemmer. There is a high degree of equality in Denmark, but it lags behind in some areas. For example, there are far more male than female top executives. This is also the case in the company where I work. It is a private company that sells IT equipment. The firm has branches in both Denmark and other countries, with around 200 employees. Even though approximately 20% of all employees are women, there are no female managers or board members.
Punkt 2: Kommentar til synspunkt
(Commentary on one prompt quote with reasons) 		Underrepræsentationen af kvinder i topledelserne i Danmark kan skyldes flere ting. Nogle mener, at en årsag kan være, at kvinder i højere grad end mænd tager ansvaret for familien og hjemmet. Det, mener jeg, er rigtigt. De fleste kvinder tager eksempelvis langt størstedelen af barselsloven, og de tager desuden et større ansvar for at få familiens dagligdag til at hænge sammen. De tager nemlig i højere grad barnets første sygedag, går på deltid for at passe børnene, henter dem fra institutioner og står for andre daglige, familiemæssige forpligtelser. Den ulige fordeling af arbejdet i hjemmet har negative konsekvenser for kvindernes karrieremuligheder, for den gør det svært samtidig at leve op til de krav og forventninger, der kan være til en leder. For eksempel at man er fleksibel og kan tage overarbejde. The underrepresentation of women in executive management in Denmark can be due to several things. Some believe that one cause may be that women, to a greater extent than men, take responsibility for family and home. This, in my view, is correct. Most women, for example, take by far the largest share of parental leave, and they also assume greater responsibility for making everyday family life work. They more often take the child's first sick day, work part-time to look after children, pick them up from daycare, and handle other daily family commitments. The unequal division of domestic labor has negative consequences for women's career prospects, because it makes it difficult to simultaneously meet the demands and expectations placed on a leader—such as being flexible and working overtime.
Punkt 3: Vurdering af fordele
(50% Rule Pt. 1: Evaluation of proposed measure's benefits) 		For at øge andelen af kvindelige topledere kunne man tage forskellige initiativer. Et forslag er at indføre kønskvoter i virksomheder, og det kan have flere fordele. Den vigtigste fordel er, at den positive særbehandling kan ændre uligheden i samfundet. Kvoter kan nemlig være med til at fjerne barrierer, der forhindrer kvinder i at få lederstillinger. Det vil medføre flere kvindelige ledere, og en mere lige kønsfordeling kan gavne virksomhederne. Mange kvinder har nemlig nogle kompetencer, som er forskellige fra mænds, og forskellighed blandt lederne kan bidrage til mere innovation og kreativitet, end hvis de alle er ens. To increase the share of female top executives, various initiatives could be taken. One proposal is to introduce gender quotas in companies, and that could offer several advantages. The most significant benefit is that affirmative action can change inequality in society. Quotas can help remove barriers that prevent women from attaining leadership positions. This will result in more female leaders, and a more balanced gender distribution can benefit companies. Many women possess competencies different from men's, and diversity among leaders can contribute to more innovation and creativity than if all are identical.
Punkt 3: Vurdering af ulemper
(50% Rule Pt. 2: Evaluation of drawbacks & counterarguments) 		På den anden side er der også nogle ulemper ved kønskvotering. En ulempe er, at kvoter kan ses som diskrimination, fordi man vælges pga. sit køn og ikke pga. sine kvalifikationer. Man prøver med andre ord at reducere kønsdiskrimination ved at diskriminere på køn, og det går imod princippet om lige muligheder for alle. En anden ulempe er, at en virksomhed måske skal ansætte en, som de ikke mener er den mest kvalificerede kandidat til stillingen. Og hvis det ikke er personen med de bedste kvalifikationer og kompetencer, der sidder i lederstillingerne, kan det skade virksomhedernes indtjening. On the other hand, there are also some drawbacks to gender quotas. One disadvantage is that quotas can be seen as discrimination because a person is chosen based on gender rather than qualifications. In other words, one attempts to reduce gender discrimination by discriminating based on gender, which contradicts the principle of equal opportunity for all. Another drawback is that a company might have to hire someone whom they do not consider the best-qualified candidate for the post. And if it is not the person with the best qualifications and skills occupying executive positions, it could harm company earnings.
🔍 6. Grammatisk Tjekliste til De Sidste 15 Minutter
The 5 Critical Proofreading Checkpoints to Fix Before Handing In Your Exam
Critical 15–20 Min

Once your essays are written, under no circumstances should you put your pen down or leave early. Spend the remaining 15–20 minutes running a line-by-line systematic sweep targeting the 5 most frequent error traps penalised by SIRI examiners:

1. Inversion (V2-reglen i helsætninger)

Check every single main clause. If the sentence does NOT start with the subject (e.g. starts with 'Desuden', 'I 2023', 'Mht. jobbet', 'Når det sker'), the finite verb MUST precede the subject:

KORREKT 'I dag arbejder (V) jeg (S)...'
FORKERT 'I dag jeg arbejder...' (English word order!)

2. Ledsætningsordstilling (S-C-V-O-reglen)

In subordinate clauses (clauses starting with conjunctions like da, fordi, som, der, hvis, selvom, at, når), the central adverbial (ikke, altid, ofte, aldrig, gerne) MUST stand BEFORE the finite verb:

Helsætning (Main clause): 'Han kommer ikke i morgen.'
KORREKT LEDSÆTNING '...fordi han IKKE (C) kommer (V) i morgen.'
FORKERT LEDSÆTNING '...fordi han kommer ikke i morgen.'

3. Nutids-r på verber (The Spise / Køre Test)

Many Danish verbs sound identical in the infinitive (navneform) and present tense (nutid) (e.g. lære vs. lærer, vurdere vs. vurderer). Substitute the verb with an audible test verb like SPISER or KØRER:

• 'Jeg skal lære' = corresponds to 'Jeg skal spise' (Infinitive → no -r)
• 'Han lærer' = corresponds to 'Han spiser' (Present tense → add -r!)
• 'At vurdere' = 'At spise' (Infinitive → no -r)
• 'Han vurderer' = 'Han spiser' (Present tense → add -r!)

4. Adjektivbøjning (T-endelse, E-endelse & Bestemthed)

Check noun-adjective concord across gender, number, and definiteness:

  • Fælleskøn singularis ubestemt (En-words): En stor mand (no suffix)
  • Intetkøn singularis ubestemt (Et-words): Et stort firma (always add -t)
  • Pluralis (All plural nouns): Store virksomheder (always add -e)
  • Bestemt form (Definite form): Den store beslutning / Det store firma (always add -e)

5. Sammensatte Navneord (Compound Nouns: No English Splitting!)

In Danish, compound nouns are written as a single combined word. Splitting them into two separate words is an English calque (særskrivning) that instantly flags the text as non-native:

KORREKT studiejob · karrieremuligheder · folkeskoleelev · kønskvoter · barselsorlov
FORKERT studie job · karriere muligheder · folkeskole elev · køns kvoter · barsels orlov
