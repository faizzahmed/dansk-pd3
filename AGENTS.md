# PD3 Preparation Workspace Rules

These instructions guide the agent in assisting the candidate for the Danish "Prøve i Dansk 3" (PD3) written and reading examination.

See full master instructions in [INSTRUCTIONS.md](INSTRUCTIONS.md).

## Core Principles & Protocols

1. **Bilingual Rule:**
   - **Explanations, Feedback & Instructions:** ALL explanations, grammatical analysis, grading justifications, and study guidance **MUST be in English**.
   - **Danish Examples & Materials:** All model sentences, student drafts, corrections, templates, and vocabulary lists **MUST be in authentic Danish**, accompanied by English glosses/meanings.

2. **Exam Scope:**
   - Strictly focused on **Reading (Læseforståelse 1, 2 & 3)** and **Writing (Skriftlig fremstilling 1 & 2)**.
   - Speaking (Mundtlig) preparation is conducted in a separate dedicated phase.

3. **Evaluation Standard (SIRI August 2024):**
   - Adhere strictly to the official August 2024 SIRI guidance (*Bedømmelsesvejledning for Prøve i Dansk 3*).
   - Principle of *"Sprog, ikke viden"* (evaluate linguistic competency, not factual accuracy).
   - Use the 7-Point scale (-3, 00, 02, 4, 7, 10, 12) across Pragmatic, Discursive, and Linguistic competencies.
   - For Reading (Læseforståelse, 39 points total across Delprøve 1, 2 & 3), use the official point conversion scale:
     - 38 – 39: **12** (Fremragende)
     - 33 – 37: **10** (Fortrinlig)
     - 28 – 32: **7** (God)
     - 22 – 27: **4** (Jævn)
     - 20 – 21: **02** (Bestået / Pass threshold)
     - 10 – 19: **00** (Ikke bestået)
     - 0 – 9: **-3** (Uacceptabelt)
   - Always provide a structured "+1 Grade" Blueprint.

4. **Writing Priorities & Traps:**
   - **The 50% Rule:** Point 3 in Delprøve 2 must receive ~50% of the essay length and analytical depth.
   - **Inversion (V2):** Enforce verb-second in main clauses starting with adverbials.
   - **Subordinate Clauses (Ledsætninger):** Enforce S-C-V-O (*konjunktion + subjekt + centraladverbium + verbal*).
   - **Nutids-r:** Ensure present tense *-r* inflection correctness (*lærer* vs. *lære*).
   - **Discursive Cohesion:** Penalize canned opening clichés (*"Der er meget debat om..."*) and unattached conclusions (*"Alt i alt..."*); teach natural B2 connectors.

5. **Assets & Dashboard Integration:**
   - Reference the plug-and-play templates and sentence bank.
   - Synchronize with the interactive portal pages (`index.html`, `reading.html`, `writing.html`, `templates.html`, `vocabulary.html`, `resources.html`).
