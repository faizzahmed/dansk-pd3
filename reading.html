⏱ 1. Læseforståelse — Ramme, Pointfordeling & Tidsallokering
Exam Framework, 39-Point Distribution & Recommended 90-Minute Pace
Total Duration: 90 Minutes (39 Points)

Manage your 90 minutes across all three parts with strict discipline:

Delprøve 1 · ~25 Min
Hurtiglæsning & Matching
Teksthæfte: 8–10 short advertisements, practical notices, or announcements (A–J).
Opgavehæfte: 7–8 matching questions. Fast scanning for exact keywords and elimination.
Delprøve 2A · ~30 Min
Dybdelæsning (Flervalg / Multiple Choice)
Teksthæfte: 2 cohesive articles/case studies (Tekst 1 & Tekst 2).
Opgavehæfte: 7 multiple-choice questions (A, B, C) · 2 points each = 14 points total. Evaluates attitudes, decisive reasons, and nuances.
Delprøve 2B · ~20 Min
Tekstsammenhæng (Huludfyldning)
Teksthæfte: A longer non-fiction article with missing sentences (huller).
Opgavehæfte: Sentence bank to insert correctly based on discursive flow, pronouns, and cohesion.
Delprøve 3 · ~15 Min
Sprog & Grammatik (Cloze Test)
Opgavehæfte: Short cohesive text with 10 word blanks. Multiple-choice lexical and grammatical test checking prepositions, collocations, and verb forms.

🎯 3. Delprøve 2A — Patterns & Exam Setup
Exam Format, Physical Structure & Common Question Archetypes
Delprøve 2A · 14 Points Total

Exam Format & Structure

Separate Documents
The reading passages are located in the Teksthæfte (text booklet), while the questions and answer options are in the Opgavehæfte (task booklet). Keep both booklets open side-by-side on your exam desk.
Text Setup (2 Related Articles)
Delprøve 2A usually consists of two related articles or personal accounts/case studies (labeled Tekst 1 and Tekst 2) centered around a broader societal, work, or lifestyle theme (such as stress, career change, volunteering, or education).
Scoring & Question Type (2 Pts Each)
The section consists of 7 multiple-choice questions (typically with options A, B, and C). Each correct answer awards 2 points, for a decisive total of 14 points out of 39.

Common Question Patterns

Questions in 2A consistently test your deep understanding of personal perspectives, main motivations, and specific opinions rather than simple factual lookup:

Pattern 1
Attitude / Opinion (Holdning / Synspunkt)
Identifying what a specific person or expert thinks about a topic.
• "Hvilken holdning giver [Navn] udtryk for?"
• "Hvilket synspunkt har [Navn] på...?"
Pattern 2
Decisive Reasons & Motives (Afgørende Grund)
Identifying the primary cause or decision factor behind an action or turning point.
• "Hvilken af følgende grunde var afgørende for...?"
• "Hvad var den afgørende grund til, at...?"
Pattern 3
Fact / Agreement Matching (Overensstemmelse)
Determining which option correctly reflects the text's contents and meaning.
• "Hvilket af følgende udsagn er i overensstemmelse med det, der står i teksten?"
⚡ 4. Delprøve 2A — Solving Strategies
Actionable Techniques to Identify Answers Rapidly and Filter Out Traps
Core Strategy
1. Scan by Person / Subheading First

Questions almost always reference a specific individual (e.g., Trine Sundsvald, Gert Jensen, Ditte Simonsen) or an expert mentioned in the article. Look for the capitalized name in the Teksthæfte to quickly locate the relevant section in seconds.

2. Watch for Paraphrasing (Avoid Word-Match Traps)

The correct option (A, B, or C) rarely uses the exact words from the reading passage. Instead, it rephrases the core idea using synonymous expressions. Distractors frequently quote verbatim snippets from unrelated sentences to mislead hasty readers.

3. Filter for the "Decisive" Reason (Afgørende Grund)

When a question asks for the afgørende grund (decisive reason), multiple options might mention things that are factually true in the text, but only one is highlighted as the main or primary motivation. Look for strong causal and priority markers in Danish (e.g., hovedårsagen, først og fremmest, vigtigst, det udslagsgivende).

🔤 5. Useful Vocabulary to Know for 2A
Key Question Stem Terms Essential for Selecting the Correct Multiple-Choice Answer
Stem Vocabulary

Understanding the precise vocabulary used in the question stems is essential for selecting the correct answer:

Nr. Danish Term / Expression English Meaning How It Is Used in 2A Questions
1 Holdning / Synspunkt Attitude / Opinion / Viewpoint "Hvilken holdning giver [Navn] udtryk for?"
Identifies subjective perspective, judgment, or stance on an issue.
2 Give udtryk for To express / state "Hvilket af følgende udsagn giver [Navn] udtryk for?"
Look for reporting verbs and quotes (mener, synes, understreger).
3 I overensstemmelse med In accordance with / Matches "Hvilket udsagn er i overensstemmelse med teksten?"
The statement must match the textual facts without contradicting or overextending.
4 Afgørende grund / årsag Decisive reason / Main cause "Hvad var den afgørende grund til, at...?"
Look for the primary catalyst that drove the action, not minor background points.
5 Konsekvens Consequence / Result "Hvad nævnes som en positiv konsekvens af...?"
Identifies direct outcomes and effects (førte til, resulterede i, betød at).
6 Hensigt / Formål Intention / Purpose "Hvad var hensigten med...?"
Focuses on what someone aimed or wanted to achieve (for at, med det formål at).
7 Overveje To consider / deliberate "Hvad overvejede [Navn] før...?"
Refers to thoughts, doubts, or options contemplated before making a decision.
8 Udsagn Statement / Claim "Hvilket af følgende udsagn...?"
Refers to the answer options (A, B, C) presenting factual claims or points.
🔄 6. Passage Flow & Question Chronology
Are the Questions Asked in the Order of the Passage?
Chronological Flow
✓ Yes, the questions in Delprøve 2A strictly follow the order of the text!

Understanding this predictable sequence eliminates wasted time looking in the wrong parts of the booklet:

Divided by Text
The question sheet explicitly groups questions by section — first listing Spørgsmål til tekst 1 (usually Questions 1–3), followed by Spørgsmål til tekst 2 (usually Questions 4–7).
Chronological Flow
Within each text group, Question 1 relates to the opening paragraph/first case study of Tekst 1, Question 2 moves further down, and Question 3 covers the end of Tekst 1. Question 4 then resets at the top of Tekst 2 and proceeds sequentially to the final question.
Efficiency Tip (Never Backtrack)
Once you find the text passage answering Question 1, you do not need to look backward in the article for Question 2 — simply continue reading forward from that point.
