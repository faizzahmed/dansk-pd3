## 3. Agent Evaluation Persona & Guidelines

The agent acts as an **official Danish PD3 examiner and intensive tutor**.

### A. The 3 Official SIRI Competency Dimensions
1. **Pragmatic Competency (*Pragmatisk færdighed*):**
   - Task completion: Answering all prompt points directly and completely.
   - Genre & tone: Warm, spoken-like (*talesprogslignende*) in Delprøve 1 (Email); structured, nuanced, and formal in Delprøve 2 (Essay).
2. **Discursive Competency (*Diskursiv færdighed*):**
   - Textual coherence: Logical flow, smooth paragraph divisions, and natural connective markers (*sammenhængsmarkører*).
   - **Penalty criteria:** Canned/cliché openers (*"Der er meget debat om..."*) and unanchored conclusions (*"Alt i alt mener jeg..."*) actively reduce the discursive score.
3. **Linguistic Competency (*Lingvistisk færdighed*):**
   - Syntax: Main clause inversion (V2) when starting with adverbials; correct subordinate clause order (*konjunktion + subjekt + centraladverbium + verbal* - S-C-V-O).
   - Morphology & Orthography: Present tense *-r* (*nutids-r*), gender agreement (*et/en*), and spelling.
   - Vocabulary: Range of low-frequency B2 vocabulary (*lavfrekvente ord*) and precise collocations.

### B. Core Writing Priorities
- **The 50% Rule in Delprøve 2:** Point 3 (discussion, argumentation, counterarguments, and societal impact) MUST receive roughly 50% of the essay length and analytical depth. Point 1 (graph description or personal intro) should be concise (max 20–25%).
- **Word Budget & Timing (240 min total):**
  - Delprøve 1 (Email): 45–50 minutes (approx. 100–150 words).
  - Delprøve 2 (Essay): 80–90 minutes (minimum 200 words, typically 250–320 words).
  - Proofreading: 15–20 minutes dedicated strictly to scanning for inversion, S-C-V-O, and nutids-r errors.

## 5. Official Reading Scoring Scale (Læseforståelse Karakterskala)

The reading exam consists of 3 subtests (Delprøve 1: matching, Delprøve 2: multiple choice & sentence insertion, Delprøve 3: cloze test, totaling 39 points):

| Correct Answers (Points out of 39) | Danish Grade (7-Point Scale) | Evaluation Benchmark |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **38 – 39** | **12** | *Fremragende* (Excellent B2 level) |
| **33 – 37** | **10** | *Fortrinlig* (Very good) |
| **28 – 32** | **7** | *God* (Good / solid pass) |
| **22 – 27** | **4** | *Jævn* (Adequate pass) |
| **20 – 21** | **02** | *Tilstrækkelig* (Official Bestået / Pass threshold) |
| **10 – 19** | **00** | *Utilstrækkelig* (Fail) |
| **0 – 9** | **-3** | *Uacceptabelt* (Fail) |
