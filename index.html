Instruktion (About this PD3 Knowledge Hub)
  • Bilingual Protocol: All explanations, time rules, strategic analyses, and examiner guidance are in English. All model sentences, scaffolds, and authentic examples are in authentic Danish with English glosses.
  • Principle of "Sprog, ikke viden": Under SIRI guidance, you are evaluated strictly on linguistic precision, coherence, and grammatical control — not on political alignment or factual accuracy.
  • The 50% Rule: For Delprøve 2 (both 2A and 2B), Point 3 (the discussion/societal perspective) must receive at least 50% of your essay length and depth.
  • Zero Monolith Design: This site is split across modular pages. Use the navigation bar above to jump directly to any topic.

⚖️ De 3 Officielle SIRI Kompetenceområder (August 2024 Standard)
How Danish Examiners evaluate your written and communicative performance
SIRI August 2024
1. Pragmatisk Færdighed (Task Completion & Genre)
• Completeness: Are all prompt questions exhaustively answered?
• Tone & Register: Warm, conversational, spoken-like tone in Delprøve 1 vs objective, balanced, and academic tone in Delprøve 2.
• The 50% Rule: Point 3 must receive ~50% of the essay length and analytical depth.
2. Diskursiv Færdighed (Cohesion & Flow)
• Textual Cohesion: Smooth paragraph transitions and logical structuring.
• Natural Connectors: Rewarding B2 markers (dermed, derimod, på den anden side).
• Cliché Penalties: Canned starters ("Der er meget debat om...") and unanchored endings ("Alt i alt...") lower the grade.
3. Lingvistisk Færdighed (Syntax, Grammar & Vocab)
• V2 Inversion: Verb-second order when starting main clauses with adverbs.
• Subordinate Clauses: S-C-V-O order in ledsætninger (conjunction + subject + central adverb + verb).
• Nutids-r: Correct present tense inflections (lærer vs lære).
• B2 Vocabulary: Low-frequency terms and precise prepositional collocations.
📈 "+1 Grade" Blueprint — Sådan løfter du din karakter
Concrete step-by-step upgrades to elevate your score from 02/4 → 7/10 → 12
Score Accelerator
Target: Grade 02 → 4 (Secure Pass)
Syntax & Task Completion
Answer every bullet point in the prompt. Eliminate catastrophic word order errors: fix V2 inversion in main clauses and avoid dropping the present-tense -r on verbs.
Target: Grade 4 → 7 (Solid Pass)
The 50% Rule & Subordinate Clauses
Expand Point 3 to half the essay length. Implement S-C-V-O word order in ledsætninger without exception. Replace simple coordination (og, men) with causal links (derfor, fordi).
Target: Grade 7 → 10/12 (High Marks)
B2 Connectors & Societal Perspective
Elevate argumentation from personal experience to broader societal perspectives (ud fra et samfundsmæssigt perspektiv). Use advanced connectors (ikke desto mindre, dermed, imidlertid).
